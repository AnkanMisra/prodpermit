# Domain glossary

## ProdPermit

The product that lets an agent investigate an incident and prepare a recovery while a person retains authority over execution.

## Recovery plan

An immutable proposal that identifies one target release, its supporting evidence, its expiry, and its fingerprint.

## Execution capability

A temporary authority to execute one approved recovery plan. It does not exist before approval and cannot execute another plan.

## Human operator

The person who reviews the recovery plan and decides whether to approve, reject, or revoke it.
